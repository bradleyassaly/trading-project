## Core Constraints (don't violate these)

1. **Reliability before scale** — no L1 → L2 promotion until 14 consecutive days with zero scheduler-task-class incidents.
2. **No live ladder progression on <30 resolved trades per category.** Statistical noise dominates below that.
3. **Promotion in one direction only on each axis per session** — never simultaneously raise bankroll AND change signal universe AND change category gate. Each ladder rung exposes one variable.
4. **All gates must symmetrically demote.** Already in `LIVE_TRADING_CHECKLIST.md`.

## Failure modes to monitor

These are the ways this plan goes off the rails. We're explicitly watching for them.

1. **Live executor still won't fire after gate audit** — means the calibration layer is broken, not the gate logic. Pivot to fixing `SignalEvaluator` before anything else.
2. **Hypothesis accuracy doesn't recover above 50% in week 2** — means the win-rate prior is wrong, not the signal mix. Re-baseline against random.
3. **Specialist boost still shows negative lift on a 30-day sample** — pull it. Don't keep a feature because it sounded right.
4. **A second silent-failure-mode shows up before May 8** — means the watchdog is still inadequate. Halt forward work; harden infra.
5. **Wallet earliness score doesn't differentiate a copyable cohort** — the wallets we're tracking may not be early; rotate the watch list.

Each of these has a specific checkpoint baked into the sprint above. None are deal-breakers for the L5 goal individually; the cumulative occurrence of three or more would force a re-plan.
